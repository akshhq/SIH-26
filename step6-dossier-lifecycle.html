The 6-State Case Lifecycle Machine

Every flagged project follows a strict state transition path ensuring legal due process and accountability:

1. TRIGGERED
Multi-Signal AI triggers Critical Risk (CRS ≥ 75). Case automatically generated with an initial Evidence Card.
2. TRIAGED
Vigilance Investigator verifies trigger parameters and issues milestone disbursement freeze lock 🔒.
3. UNDER_REVIEW
Desk audit initiated. Formal electronic explanation notice dispatched to Implementing Agency (7-day response SLA).
4. FIELD_INSPECT_ORDERED
On-site physical inspection warrant assigned to Field Verification Officer with registered GPS coordinates.
5. EVIDENCE_ATTACHED
Field Officer uploads live geotagged photographs and verifies the structural progress rung on the ground.
6. ACTION_RECOMMENDED
Final 1-click legal PDF dossier generated with SHA-256 stamp for recovery, penalty, or formal compliance clearance.

Tamper-Evident SHA-256 PDF Audit Dossier Standard

Dossier Structure

  • Executive Case Summary: Constituency, MP, Agency, Risk Score.
  • Statutory Rule Citations: Direct references to MPLADS Guidelines 2023 §3.4 and GFR 2017 Rules 130/157/238.
  • Financial Evidence Chain: Chronological ledger of all vouchers, dates, and amounts.
  • Field Photo Proofs: Geotagged site inspection photos with EXIF coordinates.
  • Cryptographic Digital Signature: SHA-256 hash ensuring legal admissibility in CAG & judicial inquiries.
