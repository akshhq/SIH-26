The Core Problem: Why Transaction Recording Fails Without Continuous AI Surveillance

The Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) handles over ₹23,353 Crore across Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha. While the existing e-SAKSHI portal records administrative milestones, it operates as a digital ledger rather than an active audit engine.

The Architectural Difference: Recording vs. Verification

A fundamental misconception in public governance software is that digitizing paper receipts prevents fraud. In reality, digitizing a fraudulent bill simply creates a digital record of a fraudulent bill.

What e-SAKSHI Does Today (Administrative Layer)

  • Records MP proposal submissions with estimated costs.
  • Generates administrative and technical sanction orders.
  • Records disbursement vouchers through the Single Nodal Account (SNA).
  • Accepts contractor invoices and claimed completion status strings.
  • The Limitation: Assumes all entered data is truthful and internally consistent.

What MPLADS Sentinel Adds (Intelligence Layer)

  • Cross-examines claims across 12 datasets in real time.
  • Checks if a contractor received 119 payments in a district (Monopoly detection).
  • Checks if 88% of funds were disbursed while physical progress is only 30%.
  • Computes semantic text embeddings to catch duplicate projects in adjacent wards.
  • Calculates calibrated 0–100 risk scores and prioritizes inspection queues.

The 5 Primary Vulnerabilities (Empirically Validated)

1. Vendor Monopoly & Coordinated Contractor Concentration

Empirical Finding in Lok Sabha: Contractor "Ajay Kumar Singh" was found receiving 119 payments from a single Member of Parliament with uniform payment amounts (~₹19,992) and near-zero variance (~10x the 99th percentile across the entire parliamentary expenditure ledger).

2. Artificial Work Splitting & Installment Structuring

Statutory Violation (GFR 2017 Rule 157): Work packages are intentionally sliced into micro-claims under ₹10 Lakh (or ₹2.5 Lakh) or disbursed in up to 52 split installments on a single Work ID to bypass open competitive tendering mandates.

3. Exact Duplicate Ledger Registrations

Data Inconsistency: Analysis of the official expenditure exports revealed 172 exact duplicate rows in Lok Sabha and 354 exact duplicate rows in Rajya Sabha with matching Work IDs, vendors, voucher dates, and claimed amounts.

4. The Physical–Financial Divergence Gap (δ)

Implementing agencies frequently draw 80% to 100% of sanctioned funds while on-site physical construction remains stalled at foundation or plinth level (<30% completed). The unspent public money sits parked in contractor accounts for years.

5. Missing & Reused Evidence Binaries

In the official Works Completed datasets, over 40% of records contain null or generic text placeholders (such as "Images") instead of verifiable photo proof, creating blindspots for ghost assets.

Why Traditional Manual Audits Cannot Scale

Under current guidelines, district authorities are mandated to inspect a random 10% sample of works annually. However, with over 45,000 active projects spread across 543 Lok Sabha constituencies and 28 States, random sampling misses 90% of irregular works.

The Sentinel Paradigm: Instead of inspecting a blind random 10%, Sentinel screens 100% of projects algorithmically, isolating the top 2% highest-risk outliers for immediate, evidence-backed on-site physical inspection.
