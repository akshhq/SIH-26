Mathematical Multi-Signal Risk Fusion & False-Positive Mitigation

How Sentinel synthesizes dozens of AI sub-scores into an objective 0–100 Composite Risk Score (CRS), prevents false alarms on honest contractors, and enforces the statutory 2-Signal Confirmation Rule.

The 8-Dimension Risk Fusion Formula

Every project's score is computed as a calibrated convex combination of 8 analytical sub-scores plus a non-linear multi-signal booster:

CRS = min( 100, ∑ [ W_i * S_i ] + Δ_multiplier )
Where ∑ W_i = 1.0, and Δ_multiplier = +15 if ≥2 independent signals exceed 80%.

Sub-Score Weights (W_i)

  • W1 (20%): Financial / Cost Anomaly Score
  • W2 (15%): Physical-Financial Divergence Gap (δ)
  • W3 (15%): Duplicate / SBERT Cloned Work Probability
  • W4 (15%): Vendor Monopoly Concentration (HHI)
  • W5 (10%): Statutory Guideline Violation Severity
  • W6 (10%): Timeline SLA Breach / Stalling Probability
  • W7 (10%): Document & Certificate Layout Similarity
  • W8 (5%): Visual Photo Reuse / Geofence Breach

Why False Positives Are Eliminated

The Problem with Simple Averages: In normal administration, a project might face a 60-day rain delay. A naive system flags it as "high risk", inundating district magistrates with false alarms.

The Sentinel Fix: A single timeline delay can ONLY raise the score by at most 10 points. To cross into Critical Risk (≥75), there MUST be independent corroborating evidence (such as 88% funds drawn + 119 payments to one contractor).

Deep Dive: Physical–Financial Divergence Gap (δ)

The Divergence Gap δ is one of Sentinel's most powerful empirical indicators:

δ = (Disbursed Amount / Sanctioned Cost * 100) - (Physical Progress %)

* If δ ≤ 10%: Healthy, synchronous milestone drawdown.
* If 15% < δ ≤ 35%: Elevated fiscal advance; queued for desk audit.
* If δ > 35%: Critical Anomaly — indicates unearned advance funds parked in contractor accounts.
