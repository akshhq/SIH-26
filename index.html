The 5-Pillar Hybrid AI Risk Architecture

Combining deterministic statutory compliance rules, unsupervised statistical anomaly detection, NLP sentence embeddings, and bipartite cartel graph theory into a calibrated 0–100 Composite Risk Score.

1

Multilingual NLP Duplicate Matcher

Identifies work splitting below ₹10L and duplicate claims using 384-dimensional dense vector embeddings (`Sentence-BERT`) and Levenshtein string-edit distance.

Sim(Wi, Wj) = 0.6·cos(ei, ej) + 0.4·LevenshteinRatio
Pillar 1 • NLP Vector Engine
2

Disparity & Stalled Project Scorer

Detects zombie projects where significant funds (80%+) were drawn but physical execution remains stagnant over elapsed timelines.

Disparity = (Expenditure/Sanction) - (PhysicalProgress%)
Pillar 2 • Temporal ML Engine
3

Category Cost Outlier Engine

Uses Modified Z-Scores (Median Absolute Deviation) and Isolation Forest to benchmark unit costs against local district category medians.

Mi = 0.6745 × (Cost_i - Median_cat) / MAD_cat
Pillar 3 • Outlier ML Engine
4

Bipartite Graph Cartel Intelligence

Builds District-to-Agency bipartite graphs (`NetworkX`) and calculates the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) to flag contractor tender monopolies.

HHI_d = Σ (Agency_Funds_k / Total_District_Funds)^2
Pillar 4 • Graph Theory Engine
5

Explainable AI (XAI) & Agentic RAG

Decomposes scores into exact percentage factors (SHAP) and cites violated clauses directly from the indexed official 2023 Guidelines PDF.

CRS = 0.25·S_dup + 0.25·S_stall + 0.20·S_cost + 0.15·S_ia + 0.15·S_rule
